My Projects

Project 1: KAIN. Campus Eats

KAIN. Campus Eats is a campus food pre-order and order management system developed for NU-Fairview. It allows students and faculty to browse food items, place orders in advance, select pickup times, and track their order status.

Vendors can manage their menus, food availability, and customer orders. The goal of this project is to reduce long queues and make ordering food on campus more convenient and organized.

Technologies Used: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Supabase, Netlify, and Power Automate.
